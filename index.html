★ Flagship Project · Machine Learning Underwriting

Credit Risk & Loan Default Analytics

Evaluated whether machine learning scoring can replace a bank's traditional "decline if credit score ≤ 620" rule across a 10,000-loan calibrated portfolio. Developed a calibrated risk scorecard with statistical hypothesis validation and profit-maximizing probability thresholding.

  • Top Model: Gradient Boosting (0.79 ROC-AUC / highest PR-AUC) vs. Random Forest & Logistic Regression.
  • Statistical Testing: Welch's t-test and Chi-square confirmed significance for DTI, utilization, loan-to-income (p < 0.001).
  • Financial Impact: Shifted test portfolio outcome from -$4.19M to +$0.14M net profit (+$4.33M swing) at optimal 0.11 default threshold.

★ Quantitative Finance · Risk & Volatility Forecasting

Multi-Asset Portfolio Risk & Volatility Forecasting

Constructed a quantitative risk engine for a 5-asset allocation (US Equities, EM Equities, Bonds, Gold, Bitcoin). Conducted Markowitz mean-variance optimization, estimated historical, parametric, and Monte Carlo VaR/CVaR, and simulated crisis correlation shocks.

  • Instability Diagnosis: Detected textbook Markowitz estimation error (100% Bitcoin allocation); engineered a deployable 35% position-capped portfolio.
  • Risk Quantification: 95% and 99% 1-day VaR on $10M book evaluated at $254K and $358K.
  • Stress Testing: Under a correlation-shock crisis regime, portfolio 1-day VaR surges by ~14%.

★ Production SQL · Database Financial Analytics

Advanced SQL for Financial Analytics

Architected six production query patterns across a 3-table banking schema (customers, accounts, transactions) to answer high-stakes institutional questions that simple aggregates cannot solve.

  • Overdraft Detection: Windowed running balances to detect intraday balance dips hidden by period-end totals.
  • Cohort Retention: Triangular monthly cohort retention matrix based on signup lifecycle.
  • Fraud & Anomaly: Rolling z-score anomaly detection using account-level historical baselines and LATERAL join velocity filters.
  • Segmentation: NTILE() Recency-Frequency-Monetary (RFM) tiers and median-based Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).
