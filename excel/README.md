# Financial KPI & Budget Variance Dashboard (Excel)

Part of the **Credit Risk & Portfolio Risk Analytics** portfolio by **Anupam Sharia**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
A live 4-tab corporate finance model built following Wall Street modeling conventions (blue fonts for hardcoded inputs, black fonts for dynamic formulas).

### Workbook Structure:
- **`Transactions`**: 420 ledger transactions across 8 expense categories and 2 revenue lines from Jan–Sep 2025.
- **`Assumptions`**: Editable budget inputs and targets. Changing any parameter here updates all downstream metrics automatically.
- **`Summary`**: Fully formula-driven (using `SUMIFS`, `INDEX`, and `MATCH`). Contains 474 verified live formulas with zero hardcoded values and zero formula errors.
- **`Dashboard`**: High-level KPI cards and native Excel charts (Actual vs. Budget Variance, Monthly Trend).

---

## 💡 How to Use
Open `Financial_KPI_Dashboard.xlsx` in Excel, Google Sheets, or LibreOffice Calc. Experiment by changing numbers in the `Assumptions` tab to watch the variance metrics recalculate in real time!
